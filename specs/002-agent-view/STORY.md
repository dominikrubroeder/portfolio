# Story: The whole portfolio as plain text

## Purpose

This portfolio says "I connect the worlds of design and code". Agent view shows the other side of that claim: the same
content, with no design at all. Anyone, or any AI tool, can read everything in one place.

## What it provides

- **One address.** `/agent-view` shows who I am, my philosophy, my tools and technologies, my projects and my
  certifications on one page of plain text.
- **No design.** No colors, fonts, motion or layout. Your theme settings change nothing. It works with scripts turned
  off.
- **Nothing hidden.** What is tucked away in drawers and tabs on the normal pages is written out in full.
- **Always in sync.** The text comes from the same place as the normal pages. Change a sentence once and both update.
- **Easy to find.** A quiet "Agent view" text link sits in the header on wide screens and just above the copyright line in
  the footer everywhere else. It never covers another control.

## Why it helps

**For the visitor:** recruiters, researchers and AI assistants get the full picture in one fast, readable page, without
clicking through drawers.

**For the site:** it shows the content is separate from its design. The same content feeds a rich interface and a plain
document, and no copy can drift apart.
