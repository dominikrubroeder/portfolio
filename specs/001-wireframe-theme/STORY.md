# Story: Two looks, one site

## Purpose

This portfolio says "I connect the worlds of design and code". The theme feature proves it. Visitors can change how the
whole site looks, and the site answers at once.

## What it provides

- **Two themes.** _Default_ is the colorful, rounded, animated look you know. _Wireframe_ shows the same site as a
  sketch: handwritten type, black-and-white images and icons, square corners, no motion, structure first. Each theme
  is a plain two-entry list.
- **One button in the header.** It opens the theme drawer with every setting in one place, light and dark mode
  included. Three quick color swatches sit next to it. No menu hunt.
- **Instant changes.** Every choice shows up immediately. No "Apply", no reload.
- **Your own tweaks.** Pick an accent color, how round the corners are, how wide the content column is, and whether
  things move.
- **Calm by default.** Wireframe starts fully still. Motion is something you switch on, never something you get.
- **Your choice is remembered.** Come back tomorrow and the site looks the way you left it, with no flash of the wrong
  look.

## Why it helps

**For the visitor:** reading is personal. Some people want a quiet page with no movement, some want strong contrast, some
want a wider column. Now they can have it in two clicks, without leaving the page.

**For the site:** it shows the design system at work. Colors, corners and motion are shared building blocks, so one
switch restyles everything. Adding a third theme later means adding one entry and its colors, not redoing pages. And because the switch
runs on plain CSS, it stays fast.
